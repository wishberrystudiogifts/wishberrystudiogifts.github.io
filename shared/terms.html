Plain words, one page.

What the page is

When you buy one of our printable cards or invitations on Etsy, your download includes a link to a personal page on this website. There you add your own words and make a link, a print or a video with them.

The files you download from Etsy are the product, and they are yours to keep. The personal page is a free extra that comes with them.

Your personal link

  • To open the page we ask for your Etsy order number. We check it with Etsy and give you a personal link.
  • The link stays open for 24 hours from the first time you open it and can be opened up to 300 times. Then the page closes.
  • While it is open, the page can make up to 100 card or invitation links.
  • Closing the page does not touch your files, and every card or invitation link you have already sent keeps working.
  • The link is for you. Please don’t share it or try to get around its limits; we may close a link that is misused.
  • Need more time? Message us on Etsy with your order number.

What we keep (privacy)

  • We do not ask for your name, email or address, and we use no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • The words you type for your card or invitation are never sent to us. They travel only inside the link you send.
  • We do not keep your order number as you typed it. We keep a code made from it, so that the same order always gets the same personal link.
  • We keep when your personal link was first and last opened, how many times, and how many links it has made.
  • For a short time (deleted within two days) we keep a code made from the internet address that tried an order number, only to stop guessing.
  • If Etsy cannot confirm an order number at that moment, the number is kept encrypted until it is confirmed — for 7 days at the latest — and is then deleted.
  • Your browser remembers your personal link and your draft on your own device, so you can come back to it.
  • The page is hosted by GitHub Pages and our small service runs on Cloudflare; both keep ordinary technical logs.
